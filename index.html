How it works

You will be shown a series of random words, one at a time, every 3 seconds.

Try to memorize as many of the words as possible. You can't go back, so give each word your full attention.

At the end of each test, you'll type every word you can remember, in any order.

You'll do this three times, with a new set of words each time. After each test you'll answer a few short questions. Just focus on the words.

When you press Start, the words will begin shortly.

Time's up

Type every word you remember. Put each one on a new line or separate them with commas. The order doesn't matter.
